# Category Boundary

AI Access Privacy is a reference category.

It exists to govern the relationship between artificial intelligence systems and the resources they are allowed to access.

Its scope begins where AI systems receive operational access.

Its scope ends before general security, privacy regulation, identity management, or AI model evaluation become primary subjects.

---

## AI Access Privacy is NOT

AI Access Privacy is not:

- an identity management framework.
- an authentication protocol.
- an authorization protocol.
- an AI safety framework.
- a cybersecurity framework.
- a privacy regulation.
- a compliance certification.
- an AI benchmarking system.
- an access control product.
- a replacement for Zero Trust.
- a replacement for OAuth.
- a replacement for IAM.

---

## AI Access Privacy DOES

AI Access Privacy defines how AI access itself can be:

- described
- declared
- verified
- observed
- governed
- monitored
- revoked

independently of the technologies used to implement access.

---

## Relationship to Existing Disciplines

Authentication answers:

Who are you?

Authorization answers:

What are you allowed to do?

Identity answers:

Who is acting?

Privacy answers:

What obligations exist?

Security answers:

How do we reduce risk?

AI Governance answers:

How should organizations govern AI?

AI Access Privacy answers:

Under what governed conditions may AI systems interact with protected resources throughout their operational lifecycle?

---

AI Access Privacy complements these disciplines.

It does not replace them.
